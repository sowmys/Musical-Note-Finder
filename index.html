Pitch trail · last 8 seconds

Start listening, then record up to 20 seconds of singing.

Shaded lines are the swaras of the chosen raga. A wavy trace between lines is a gamaka; that is normal in Carnatic singing. Use headphones if you play a drone from another device, so the listener hears only your voice.

Check accuracy

Both tests play the chosen raga's arohanam at your Sa, with known exact frequencies, and compare what the listener detects. The first feeds the notes straight into the detector, so it tests the maths. The second plays them out loud and listens with the microphone, so it tests the real path your voice takes. Keep the room quiet and the volume up for the second one.
